About Centific

We partner with local communities, organizations, and experts to ensure our studies are meaningful and impactful. Your participation helps create healthier, stronger communities.

Frequently Asked Questions

These projects aim to gather valuable insights to improve health and community wellness through innovative research. Our studies focus on real-world applications to enhance technologies and services that benefit communities globally.
Click on the 'Learn More' button for each project to see details and sign up. You’ll be guided through a simple registration process, and our team will contact you with further details.
Eligibility criteria vary by project. Some may require specific age groups, locations, or other qualifications. Please check the details of each project for specific requirements.
We prioritize your privacy. All data is securely stored, and participants will review a detailed privacy and consent form before any session. Your information is never shared without your consent.
After signing up, you’ll receive a confirmation email with next steps, including scheduling details and any preparation needed. Our team will guide you through the process.
Our projects require collecting data in realistic at-home environments to ensure accurate and natural results. We use Airbnb to rent diverse home settings, allowing participants to contribute from authentic residential spaces. All rentals are managed directly through our company to ensure a secure and efficient process.
